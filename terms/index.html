Terms & Health Disclaimer

Apex for iPhone · Last updated 13 September 2026

Apex tells you what to lift and how much. It is a training tool, not a doctor, and it does not know anything about your body that you have not typed into it. Read this before you use it.

Health & injury disclaimer

Apex is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice. It is a general fitness tool for healthy adults. The weights, repetitions and programmes it suggests are generated from the training history you enter, and it has no knowledge of your health, injuries, medication or physical limits. Consult a qualified physician before beginning any exercise programme, and particularly if you have a medical condition, an existing or previous injury, are pregnant, or have not trained recently. Strength training carries an inherent risk of serious injury. Stop immediately and seek medical attention if you experience pain, dizziness, chest tightness or shortness of breath. Every prescription Apex makes is a suggestion, never an instruction — the decision to attempt any lift is yours alone, and you use Apex entirely at your own risk.

Some practical additions, which apply on this page only and need not travel with the paragraph above:

  • Train within your ability. Use a spotter or set the safety pins when you are working near a limit, especially on squats and bench press.
  • A suggested weight is derived from what you logged last time, nothing more. If it looks wrong, it is wrong — trust yourself over the app.
  • Apex cannot detect form breakdown, fatigue, illness or injury. Only you can.
  • Nothing in the app is a diagnosis, a treatment, or a substitute for professional medical or coaching advice.
