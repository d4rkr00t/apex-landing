Support

Apex for iPhone · Last updated 13 September 2026

Apex is made by one person. Email apex@sysoev.org and you will get a reply from that person, usually within a few days. Bug reports, feature requests and complaints are all welcome.

If you are reporting a bug, the useful things to include are your iPhone model, your iOS version, and what you did just before it went wrong. A screenshot beats a description.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up, no email address, no password, and nothing to log in to. Your training data lives on your iPhone from the moment you enter it.

Does it work without a connection?

Yes, completely. Apex is built to be used in a basement gym with no signal — every feature works offline and nothing waits on a network.

How do I move my history to a new iPhone?

Turn on iCloud backup in Settings before you switch. It is off by default, so this is something you have to opt into; once on, Apex keeps up to seven daily backups in your own private iCloud container. You can also export your entire history to a file from Settings and move it across yourself.

What does Apex do with Apple Health?

If you allow it, Apex writes a workout record — the duration and an estimate of active energy — when you finish a session. It never reads anything from Health, and it does not request permission to. You can revoke access at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Apex.

How long is the free trial?

Fourteen days. The trial is tied to your Apple ID rather than to the installation, so deleting the app and installing it again continues the same trial rather than starting a new one.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by the developer, at reportaproblem.apple.com. Emailing me cannot make one happen faster. Be aware that once a purchase is refunded or a subscription lapses, Apex locks immediately — that is deliberate.

How do I cancel a subscription?

On your device: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. The developer has no access to your subscription and cannot cancel it for you.

Is my training data sent anywhere?

No. See the privacy policy for the full detail, including the two optional cases — Apple Health and iCloud backup — where data leaves the app because you asked it to.
